HMRC tax adviser registration

Do you need to register as a tax adviser?

If you're paid to deal with HMRC about someone else's tax, HMRC now requires you to register for an agent services account. Here are the dates, and the anti-money laundering supervision you need first.

Short answer: If you interact with HMRC about someone else's tax affairs and get paid for it, HMRC treats you as a tax adviser and you need an agent services account, even if you don't call yourself a tax adviser. You must have anti-money laundering (AML) supervision in place before you apply. Source: HMRC (opens in new tab).

Registration windows

HMRC is bringing registration in by group. Each group has 3 months from the date its window opens. HMRC manual MTAR10800 (opens in new tab).

  • 18 May to 17 August 2026: new tax advisers, or those without an agent services account, Self Assessment or Corporation Tax account.
  • 18 August to 17 November 2026: tax advisers with a Self Assessment or Corporation Tax agent account but no agent services account.
  • 18 November 2026 to 17 February 2027: advisers who only provide payroll services and don't have an agent services account.
  • 31 December 2026 to 31 March 2027: financial services organisations without an agent services account.

The payroll window only applies if payroll is all you do. If you also do other tax or accounts work with HMRC, an earlier window applies. HMRC (opens in new tab). If you missed your window or are new, HMRC says register now. HMRC (opens in new tab).

You can keep dealing with HMRC for clients during your 3 months and while HMRC considers your registration. If you already have an agent services account, you don't need to register again. HMRC (opens in new tab).

Who has to register

Dealing with HMRC includes phone, post or email, messages through GOV.UK or the HMRC app, making payments, and sending returns, claims or other documents. You need to register even if you work as a sole trader, tax isn't your main business, or you only have one client. The business registers, not each employee. HMRC (opens in new tab).

You don't need to register if, for example, you only run payroll for your own staff, help friends or family for free, only reply to an HMRC request for information, or only make accounting or payroll software. Other exceptions include customs and import VAT, and appeals to a court or tribunal. See HMRC's full list (opens in new tab), or use HMRC's checker tool on the same page.

AML supervision comes first

Get supervised before you apply. HMRC says you must have approved anti-money laundering supervision in place before you apply for an agent services account, and it will reject applications without it. HMRC (opens in new tab).

When you register, you'll give the name of your AML supervisory body, your membership number and renewal date. HMRC (opens in new tab). Accepted evidence includes your supervision certificate, a renewal email or letter, proof of payment, or a screenshot of your entry on your supervisor's public register. HMRC (opens in new tab).

Under the Money Laundering Regulations, tax advisers, accountants and payroll agents who give accountancy services or tax advice are accountancy service providers. They must register with HMRC for AML supervision unless a professional body already supervises them. HMRC (opens in new tab).

AML supervisors for accountants, bookkeepers and tax advisers

GOV.UK lists these as the main supervisory bodies. If one of them supervises you, you don't register with HMRC for AML. HMRC (opens in new tab).

  • Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT)
  • Association of Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA)
  • Association of International Accountants (AIA)
  • Association of Taxation Technicians (ATT)
  • Chartered Institute of Management Accountants (CIMA)
  • Chartered Institute of Taxation (CIOT)
  • Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)
  • Institute of Certified Bookkeepers (ICB)
  • Institute of Chartered Accountants in Ireland (ICAI)
  • Institute of Chartered Accountants of Scotland (ICAS)
  • Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales (ICAEW)
  • Institute of Financial Accountants (IFA)
  • Institute of Accountants and Bookkeepers
  • Law Society

Membership of a professional body doesn't always include AML supervision, and some charge separately for it, so check with the body. HMRC manual ECSH52575 (opens in new tab). The full list of supervisory authorities (opens in new tab) also includes legal bodies. Listed in GOV.UK's order. Nobody pays to be listed here.

HMRC's AML fees

If HMRC supervises you, it charges (from 1 December 2025): HMRC (opens in new tab)

  • £300 one-off application fee
  • £400 for each premises when you register, then £400 per premises each year (annual declaration fee)
  • £40 approval test for each relevant owner, officer or manager

If you have no business address, your home or contact address counts as premises. HMRC (opens in new tab). If your turnover is under £5,000 you can apply for a small business reduction: you pay in full and get £500 back once accepted. HMRC (opens in new tab). Professional bodies set their own fees; ask them.

Other conditions

Your business mustn't, for example, have relevant outstanding returns or unpaid tax (unless on a payment plan), unspent convictions for fraud or tax offences, or be insolvent. If your business has 5 officers or fewer, all of them must meet the conditions too. HMRC (opens in new tab).

If you don't register

You may not be able to deal with HMRC for your clients, and you may face sanctions. HMRC says it will give you a chance to put things right first. HMRC (opens in new tab). Registration itself is free. HMRC (opens in new tab).

Questions

Frequently asked

Is tax adviser registration the same as Making Tax Digital?

No. It's a separate HMRC requirement for people paid to deal with HMRC for clients. HMRC (opens in new tab). For MTD, use our checker.

Does it cost anything to register?

Registration with HMRC is free, but you need AML supervision first, which has its own fees. HMRC (opens in new tab).

I already have an agent services account. Do I need to do anything?

You don't need to register again. HMRC will contact you through your account if it needs more information. HMRC (opens in new tab).

I only do payroll for clients. When do I register?

From 18 November 2026, with 3 months to apply, if payroll is the only way you deal with HMRC. HMRC (opens in new tab).
