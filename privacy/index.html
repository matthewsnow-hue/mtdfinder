Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-18.

Who we are

MTD Finder (mtdfinder.co.uk) is run by Matthew Snow trading as Sno Automations, a sole trader based in the UK. For the purposes of UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), Matthew Snow is the data controller. Contact: hello@mtdfinder.co.uk. Matthew Snow is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number ZC276141. You can check this on the ICO register (opens in new tab).

Browsing the site

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and this site sets no cookies of its own. The MTD checker runs entirely in your browser: the figures you type are never sent to us or stored.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit, GitHub processes technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site, and may log it. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (Google LLC), so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. These providers may process data outside the UK under their own safeguards. See GitHub's privacy statement (opens in new tab) and Google's privacy policy (opens in new tab). Our lawful basis is legitimate interests in running a secure, working website.

Visitor statistics

We use GoatCounter to count visits. It sets no cookies and doesn't store IP addresses or build profiles of visitors. It tells us totals such as which pages are read and which sites sent visitors. GoatCounter is run from Ireland and stores its data in Finland and Germany. Our lawful basis is legitimate interests in understanding how the site is used. See GoatCounter's privacy notes (opens in new tab).

If you email us

If you contact us, we use your email address and message to reply, on the basis of legitimate interests. Our email is provided by Google Workspace. We keep correspondence only as long as needed, normally no more than two years.

Sponsors and payments

If you buy a featured listing, payment is taken by Stripe, which collects your card details directly. We never see your full card number. We receive your name, business name, email, billing address and payment status, and use them to provide the listing, invoice and keep accounts (lawful basis: contract and legal obligation). Accounting records are kept for at least six years, as UK tax law requires. See Stripe's privacy policy (opens in new tab).

Newsletter

If you sign up to the newsletter, we use your email address (and your name, if you give it) to send it, on the basis of your consent. We'll ask you to confirm your subscription by email first. Sign-ups and our mailing list are managed by Kit (ConvertKit LLC), a US-based email service that processes the data for us as our processor under its data processing addendum (opens in new tab). Transfers of UK personal data to the US are protected by the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses. Kit may record whether you open or click our emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email hello@mtdfinder.co.uk to unsubscribe. When you unsubscribe, we delete your address, or keep only a suppression record so we don't email you again. See Kit's privacy policy (opens in new tab).

Business contacts we approach

Now and then we email organisations and businesses (for example landlord and trade associations, bookkeeping bodies and software vendors) to offer free resources, check listing details or discuss sponsorship. We use only business contact details they publish themselves, such as a role inbox or contact form, on the basis of legitimate interests in telling relevant organisations about the site. We keep a record of who we contacted and when, and if you ask us not to contact you again, we add you to a suppression list and won't. Contact: hello@mtdfinder.co.uk.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict your data, object to processing, ask for a copy to take elsewhere, or withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@mtdfinder.co.uk; we'll reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (opens in new tab).

Changes

We'll update this page if anything changes, and show the date at the top.
