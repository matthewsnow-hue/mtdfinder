Who we are

MTD Finder is run by Matthew Snow trading as Sno Automations, a UK sole trader, who is the data controller. Contact: hello@mtdfinder.co.uk.

What we collect

The MTD checker runs entirely in your browser. The figures you enter are never sent to us. We don't currently use analytics or advertising cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

Newsletter (when live)

If you sign up, we'll store your email address to send the newsletter from newsletter@mtdfinder.co.uk, on the basis of your consent. You can unsubscribe at any time and we'll delete your address.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
