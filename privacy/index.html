Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-14.

Who we are

MTD Finder (mtdfinder.co.uk) is run by Matthew Snow trading as Sno Automations, a sole trader based in the UK. For the purposes of UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018), Matthew Snow is the data controller. Contact: hello@mtdfinder.co.uk.

Browsing the site

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and this site sets no cookies of its own. The MTD checker runs entirely in your browser: the figures you type are never sent to us or stored.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit, GitHub processes technical information such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site, and may log it. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts (Google LLC), so your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. These providers may process data outside the UK under their own safeguards. See GitHub's privacy statement (opens in new tab) and Google's privacy policy (opens in new tab). Our lawful basis is legitimate interests in running a secure, working website.

If you email us

If you contact us, we use your email address and message to reply, on the basis of legitimate interests. Our email is provided by Google Workspace. We keep correspondence only as long as needed, normally no more than two years.

Sponsors and payments

If you buy a featured listing, payment is taken by Stripe, which collects your card details directly. We never see your full card number. We receive your name, business name, email, billing address and payment status, and use them to provide the listing, invoice and keep accounts (lawful basis: contract and legal obligation). Accounting records are kept for at least six years, as UK tax law requires. See Stripe's privacy policy (opens in new tab).

Newsletter

If you sign up to the newsletter, we store your email address and send it from newsletter@mtdfinder.co.uk, on the basis of your consent. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us to unsubscribe. When you unsubscribe, we delete your address, or keep only a suppression record so we don't email you again. We'll update this section to name any sign-up provider before sign-ups open.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict your data, object to processing, ask for a copy to take elsewhere, or withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@mtdfinder.co.uk; we'll reply within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (opens in new tab).

Changes

We'll update this page if anything changes, and show the date at the top.
