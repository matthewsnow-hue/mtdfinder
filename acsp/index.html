Before you choose an agent

This list isn't complete. Companies House says the agents on it "have requested to have their details published. It may not include all agents that have registered as an ACSP." It also says it doesn't update the list on a set schedule, and that it "does not endorse or recommend the agents on this list". Companies House (opens in new tab).

  • Ask your own accountant first. If you already have an accountant or solicitor, Companies House suggests asking them whether they're registered as an ACSP. Companies House (opens in new tab).
  • Check they haven't been ceased or suspended. An agent on the ceased or suspended list (opens in new tab) can't act as an ACSP. That list can be up to 2 weeks behind, so we remove anyone on it each time we update. Companies House (opens in new tab).
  • Ask what they charge and how they check. Companies House suggests asking agents directly what services they offer, what they charge, their terms, and whether checks are in person or remote. Companies House (opens in new tab).
  • Check their anti-money laundering supervisor. Each listing shows one. If it's HMRC, you can check HMRC's register of supervised businesses (opens in new tab). If it's a professional body, check with that body.

You may not need an agent at all

You can verify yourself online with GOV.UK One Login if you have accepted photo ID, such as a biometric passport or a UK photo driving licence. There's also a route if you don't have those. Companies House (opens in new tab).

When you need to verify

Identity verification became a legal requirement from 18 November 2025. Companies House says that date "is not a deadline". It started a 12-month transition period. Existing directors give their personal code as part of the company's next confirmation statement, and people with significant control (PSCs) have their own 14-day period. Companies House (opens in new tab), when you need to verify (opens in new tab).

What the agent needs from you

Your full name, home address, email address and evidence to prove your identity. Companies House then emails your personal code to the address you gave your ACSP. Companies House (opens in new tab).

Other ways to find an accountant

Professional bodies run their own directories. These list members, not ACSPs, so ask anyone you find there whether they're registered as an ACSP.

  • ICAEW: find a chartered accountant (opens in new tab)
  • ACCA: find an accountant (opens in new tab)
  • AAT: find an accountant or bookkeeper (opens in new tab)
  • CIOT: find a Chartered Tax Adviser (opens in new tab)
  • ATT: find an ATT member (opens in new tab)

Questions

Frequently asked

What is an ACSP?

An Authorised Corporate Service Provider, also known as a Companies House authorised agent. It's an agent, such as an accountant or solicitor, registered with Companies House and supervised for anti-money laundering. Companies House (opens in new tab).

Is this the full list of ACSPs?

No. Companies House only publishes agents who asked to be listed, so it may not include every ACSP. Companies House (opens in new tab).

Do I have to use an ACSP?

No. You can verify yourself online with GOV.UK One Login if you have accepted photo ID. Companies House (opens in new tab).

Is this the same as Making Tax Digital?

No. Companies House identity verification is separate from HMRC's Making Tax Digital for Income Tax. For MTD, use our checker.
