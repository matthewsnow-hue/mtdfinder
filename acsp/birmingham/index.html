ACSPs in Birmingham

Authorised agents on Companies House's public list for Birmingham as the town. Independent, and not run by Companies House.

Short answer: An ACSP (Authorised Corporate Service Provider), also known as a Companies House authorised agent, is an agent such as an accountant or solicitor who is registered with Companies House and supervised for anti-money laundering. You can ask one to verify your identity for Companies House. Source: Companies House (opens in new tab).

45 agents on Companies House's public list match Birmingham as the town (list published 12 November 2026, synced 16 November 2026). Agents are included where Companies House's town field is Birmingham.

Nobody pays to be on this list or to appear higher: it is listed A to Z. The official list may be incomplete, and Companies House does not endorse or recommend the agents on it. We are not endorsed by Companies House or HMRC.

Before you choose an agent

  • Check they aren't on Companies House's ceased or suspended list (opens in new tab). If they are, they can't act as an ACSP. Companies House (opens in new tab).
  • Contact agents directly, or check their website, to find out what services they offer, what they charge, their terms and conditions, and whether they need to do checks in person or can work remotely. Companies House (opens in new tab).
  • You may not need an agent. You can verify yourself online with GOV.UK One Login if you have accepted photo ID. Companies House (opens in new tab).
