MTD for landlords

What counts, how joint ownership works, and which software fits.

Short answer: If your gross property income (plus any self-employment income) is over the threshold, you need MTD. For a jointly owned property, only your share counts. Source: HMRC (opens in new tab).

Rental income is measured before expenses

Qualifying income is your total self-employment and property income before expenses. HMRC's own example: £25,000 rental income plus £27,000 self-employment income gives qualifying income of £52,000. HMRC (opens in new tab).

Jointly owned property

Your share counts. HMRC's example: a property generating £50,000 shared equally with a sibling gives you qualifying income of £25,000. If you only get notice of your share after expenses, HMRC uses that figure. HMRC (opens in new tab).

Foreign property

If you were UK tax resident, foreign property income counts too. HMRC (opens in new tab). Filter the finder for landlord products, and check foreign property support on each HMRC listing.

One product per submission

You can use more than one product, but not more than one to send quarterly updates for the same property income. HMRC (opens in new tab).

Questions

Frequently asked

Do landlords need Making Tax Digital?

Yes, if you're registered for Self Assessment and your qualifying income from property and self-employment is over the threshold. HMRC (opens in new tab).

Does my partner's half of a rental count?

No. Only your share of jointly owned property income counts towards your qualifying income. HMRC (opens in new tab).

Do REIT dividends count?

No. Income from UK REITs or PAIFs does not count towards qualifying income. HMRC (opens in new tab).
