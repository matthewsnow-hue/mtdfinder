MTD deadlines and quarterly dates

When you start, and when each update is due.

Short answer: Quarterly updates are due by 7 August, 7 November, 7 February and 7 May. Your tax return is due by 31 January after the tax year. Source: HMRC.

6 April to 5 April7 May (the following tax year)

If your accounting period runs 1 April to 31 March, HMRC suggests you consider calendar update periods, which end on the last day of the month. The deadline to send each update is the same either way. You select calendar periods in your software before your first update. HMRC: send quarterly updates.

Quarterly updates are summaries of your income and expenses. They are not tax returns. HMRC.

Your tax return

You add any other income and submit your tax return through your software by 31 January the following year. HMRC: submit your tax return.

Questions

Frequently asked

When is the first MTD quarterly update due?

For the period 6 April to 5 July, the deadline is 7 August. HMRC.

Is a quarterly update a tax return?

No. HMRC describes quarterly updates as summaries of your self-employment and property income and expenses, not tax returns. HMRC.

Can I use calendar quarters?

Yes. If your accounting period covers 1 April to 31 March you can choose calendar update periods in your software; deadlines stay the same. HMRC.
